## Findings supported by the repository

1. **hondaPico is a reference, not a qualified logger supply.**
   `hondaPico-kicad/gen_sch.py:650` and its BOM contain the LM2678 stage,
   22 uH inductor and 820 uF output capacitor. `circuits.md:1021` records the
   fitted 100 uH axial and unverified saturation rating. The new logger
   allocation cannot inherit load validation from that assembly history.

2. **The firmware work is larger than adding an elapsed-time condition.**
   `picow-logger.ino:12` assigns SD/network to core 0; `setup1()` at line 367
   assigns inputs and reporting to core 1. Lines 403–405 wait up to 3 s for
   USB. The `l` command at line 442 blocks for 200 ms and command processing
   at line 572 can execute multiple commands per burst. ADC sampling and
   output work also occur in the reporting path. A loop-top poll alone
   therefore cannot meet the proposed 10 ms power-service interval.
   The source has no application HOLD/watchdog implementation.

3. **Shutdown needs a new cross-core interface.** `logger_sd.h` exposes
   setup/loop/status operations, not a producer-stop/drain/close handshake.
   `OutTee` and its partial-line buffer belong to core 1, while SdFat belongs
   to core 0. A completion after IGN cancels shutdown needs state/generation
   checking. A 4 s watchdog period is not the whole fault-to-off bound when
   feeding eligibility permits 2 s-old progress.

4. **Automatic off-state isolation is still design work.** The ADC interface
   drawing puts upper clamps on switched 3V3; removing the Pi alone does not
   remove those paths. Replacement interfaces must preserve input loading,
   pulse behavior, analog range and ground-reference integrity. Specify
   permitted pin voltages and leakage as well as rail voltage: a small rail
   reading alone is not a complete powered-off interface acceptance criterion.
   Treat historical "zero leakage" prose in the fuel spec in light of the
   newer caveat at its top and `fuel/NEXT_SESSION.md`; do not inherit it as
   proof. Fuel-owned corrections go to that owner as patches.

5. **The independent timer is required by the draft policy but absent from
   the circuit.** The next increment needs an actual timer, power-on/reset
   behavior, IGN qualification, latch and series HOLD-path interruption.
   It must survive logger reset and refuse rearming from HOLD or ordinary
   firmware activity. Specify recovery from its own brownout with IGN absent.
   QP drain-source short and IGN stuck high remain separate uncovered faults;
   do not silently expand the timer's claimed coverage to include them.

6. **Current documentation does not resolve protection and procurement.**
   CIN/COUT still lack exact series; the timer, isolation and vehicle
   protection are placeholder BOM entries. The upstream hardware record
   documents purchases, not present spare inventory. The standalone inventory
   files are not part of this checkout. No ordering or vehicle readiness is
   established by this review.

Manufacturer checks support retaining the open gates:

- LM2678 standby is 50 uA typical, 100 uA maximum at 25 C and 150 uA
  maximum over its specified junction-temperature range. Its current-limit
  upper bound reaches 8.75 A. Output capacitance and ESR affect stability.
  These support the draft's EN comparison, fault-current concern and need
  for an exact capacitor choice. [TI LM2678, electrical characteristics and
  application guidance](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/lm2678.pdf).
- The 1.5KE30A table specifies 41.4 V clamping at its stated pulse current
  and test conditions, not a universal ceiling for the installation. Its
  use still needs coordination with the regulator, drivers and harness.
  [Vishay 1.5KE series](https://www.vishay.com/docs/88301/15ke.pdf).
