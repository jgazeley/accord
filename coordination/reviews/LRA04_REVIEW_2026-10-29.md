## Findings

### R04-REV-1 — the shutdown current is unreachable in the proposed topology (high)

`INTERFACE_PROPOSAL` proposes "GP10 DE//RE (tied with 10 kΩ pulldown
default-listen, matching keyless style)" and, four lines later, "Enable/reset
defaults: DE low + /RE high = shutdown". Those are mutually exclusive. With DE
and RE on one net — which `keyless/hardware/hondaPico.kicad_sch` does carry, both
pins labelled `RS485_DE` — a low level enables the receiver and a high level
enables the driver. The low-power state the return costs at 2 nA cannot be
entered at all.

Resolve one of two ways, and say which: spend a second GPIO so DE and RE are
driven separately and the shutdown state exists, or keep the single tied line
and drop every claim that depends on shutdown mode, costing the quiescent
receiver current instead. The keyless precedent is a tied line; the logger's
allocation is proposed separately and may legitimately differ.

### R04-REV-2 — the off-state recommendation contradicts the return's own finding (high)

The RS-485 section records that with V<sub>CC</sub> = 0 the bus pins draw up to
1 mA at 12 V and −0.8 mA at −7 V, and correctly says this "must fit 2/5 µA
allocation or request tradeoff". The recommendation then calls RS-485 the "best
fit to 2/5 µA provisional allocation with proper enable". Both cannot stand.

The allocation governs the parked board with the bus possibly still energized,
so a switched rail does not by itself answer it; the transceiver's own supply
current is not the quantity in question. Name the isolation or disconnection
measure that makes the number hold, or state plainly that the allocation is
unmet pending R05 and carry it as the option's leading open risk.

### R04-REV-3 — WITHDRAWN. The return was right and this review was wrong (high)

**Withdrawn October 29 after the owner supplied the evidence.** The original
finding said "proven on keyless" overstated the case. It did not. The return was
correct and this review was wrong twice: first guessing the transceiver was
unpopulated, then conceding it was fitted but calling bus operation unrecorded.

Owner, October 29: the keyless MAX3485 is fitted in an IC socket, and the
RS-485 link **was tested extensively** from the Pi 4 with a host CLI — commands
of the form `./hp out lock pulse`, `./hp io`, `./hp status`.

The tooling exists and is readable, outside this repository:

| Path | What it is |
|---|---|
| `/srv/agent_share/pcb/benchlink/hp` | 17-line shim. Auto-detects `/dev/ttyUSB*`, execs `cm5sim` |
| `/srv/agent_share/pcb/benchlink/cm5sim` | The real tool. Stands in for the CM5, the bus's only initiator |

What that establishes, which no document in this repository previously did:

- **The bus was exercised at 115200 with a real protocol**, not a loopback:
  `0xA5 dst type len payload crc8`, with PING/PONG, STATUS, IO, ENROL and OUT
  against channels `lock unlock trunk flasher horn sec_led ceiling`. NAK codes
  including "ignition off" and "interlock refused" imply round trips that
  reached application logic, not just framing.
- **The host end used a Waveshare SP485EEN + FT232RNL adapter** with hardware
  automatic direction control, so the host drove no DE line. The Pico end did:
  `link.h:221` raises GP10, waits 10 µs for driver enable, sends, flushes, then
  holds one character time plus margin before dropping DE, explicitly to avoid
  truncating the last stop bit. That is a worked and exercised half-duplex
  implementation, not a sketch.

Consequence for the corrected pass: the keyless precedent is **operational
evidence**, and the strongest single argument for the RS-485 recommendation.
Say so. It also bears on R04-REV-1 — the tied-DE topology is proven in service,
so that finding asks only for the unreachable shutdown claim to be dropped, not
for the topology to be reconsidered.

**Why this review got it wrong, recorded so the next one does not repeat it.**
Both wrong versions came from reading
`keyless/firmware/accord-keyless/accord-keyless.ino` lines 5–6 — "works today,
with no MAX3485 fitted", "once U5 is soldered" — as current fact. The packet
warns in as many words to "confirm populated hardware and operational bus
evidence separately from stale firmware comments". The review quoted that
warning while doing the opposite with it. `cm5sim`'s own docstring carries the
same stale line. Firmware comments in this project date from when they were
written; the owner is the authority on what is physically built and run.

### R04-REV-4 — citations do not meet the packet's standard (medium)

The packet requires "current primary datasheets/documentation with version/date
and links for factual claims". The return names sources without revision, date
or URL, so none of its datasheet figures — the 2 nA shutdown typical, the 1 µA
maximum, the common-mode range, the MCP2515 active current — can be checked as
returned. This manager has not verified any of them and does not dispute them;
they are simply unsupported at present.

It also cites "SIT65HVD230", which is not a part number this review can place.
The 3.3 V CAN transceiver the description fits is TI's SN65HVD230. Correct the
designator or name the actual manufacturer and document.

### R04-REV-5 — the CAN option is not actually allocated (medium)

The packet asks for exact GPIO/peripheral use and for pin conflicts with
concrete resolutions. "SPI1, avoid SPI0 and the reserved set" is not an
allocation. On RP2040 the SPI1 candidate pins overlap GP8/GP9/GP10 — the very
pins this same return proposes for RS-485 — and the remaining ADC-capable GP28
is kept free by `REV_A_INTERFACES.md` §1. Either name the pins the CAN option
would take, including the interrupt line and oscillator provision, or state that
CAN cannot be allocated without a pin trade and say which trade.

### R04-REV-6 — reconcile against the integrated LRA-03 pin table (medium)

LRA-03 was integrated at `dc07292` after this return was written.
`logger/hardware/as-built/REV_A_INTERFACES.md` §1 is now the current proposed
pin table: GP8/GP9/GP10 are explicitly offers "not reserved until LRA-04
agrees", GP11–GP15 and GP20–GP22 are unassigned with at least two to be brought
to pads, and GP28 is kept free as the only remaining ADC channel. Reference that
table and state whether the proposal accepts the offer as made. The coordination
the return defers to LRA-03 is available now.
