### R03-REV-3 — correct GP5 thresholds and distinguish the two circuits (medium)

`AS_BUILT.md:305` and `check_as_built_numbers.py:138` use `0.65*3.3` as
RP2040 VIH. Table 625 gives **2.0 V at IOVDD=3.3 V**; the `0.65*IOVDD`
formula belongs to the **1.8 V** row. VIH is a guaranteed input-level boundary,
not a measured switching threshold. [RP2040 datasheet, section 5.5.3.4](https://datasheets.raspberrypi.com/rp2040/rp2040-datasheet.pdf).

For an ideal 100 nF charged from zero to a nominal 3.3 V rail, times to 2.0 V
are 4.658/5.589/7.452 ms for 50/60/80 kΩ. The separate calculations to the
contract's stricter 3.0 V level (11.99–19.18 ms internally, 2.40 ms at 10 kΩ)
are arithmetically sound under their assumptions. They do not establish a
worst-case timing qualification or by themselves prove a firmware defect.

The as-built opto collector drives GP5; Rev A disconnects that path, moves the
opto output to the A5 qualification network, and uses QREPORT to drive GP5
(`CUTOFF_MARGIN_REVIEW.md` section 2). State explicitly where the old 100 nF
would go, if retained. QREPORT, not the optocoupler, then sinks the GP5 pull-up.
Keep the need to budget report-node delay plus software observation, but make
retention of that capacitor a proposal and include rail/component/leakage
corners before claiming timing closure. The existing external 10k remains the
power contract's candidate; no pin or topology replacement is requested here.

### R03-REV-4 — do not turn conditional opto arithmetic into a guarantee (high)

`AS_BUILT.md:433` calls 50% CTR a worst-case bin and infers saturation with
43x/7.2x margin. Sharp's published 50% minimum is conditioned on IF=5 mA,
VCE=5 V and Ta=25 C; it is not a saturation guarantee at ~4.72 mA, hot operation
or an unidentified kit optocoupler. [Sharp PC817XxNSZ1B datasheet](https://global.sharp/products/device/lineup/data/pdf/datasheet/PC817XxNSZ1B_e.pdf).

Moreover, the Rev A opto drives IGN_A/IGN_RC, not the 10k GP5 pull-up. Label
the current estimates as conditional screens and use the actual Rev A load
for endpoint analysis. Retain 2.2k as a sensible electrical-review input, but
change the claim at line 449 that it already solves the protection defect:
`28^2/2200 = 0.356 W` is nominal arithmetic, not proof of the fitted resistor's
wattage, tolerance, hot derating or pulse capability. R02/R03 remain open.
