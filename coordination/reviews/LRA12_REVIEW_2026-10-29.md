## Findings

### R12-REV-1 — the clamp chain's premise is false (revision requested)

`LAYOUT_CLOSURE.md` §3.1 step 2 and §3.4 option (c) rest on "the only
P-channel candidate with a hot zero-gate drain-current maximum is
IPD50P04P4L-11", rated 40 V, so a ≥45 V switch with a hot I<sub>DSS</sub>
maximum "was not found".

Vishay **SQD50P06-15L** (document 69098, S12-2006 Rev. E,
[datasheet](https://www.vishay.com/docs/69098/sqd50p06.pdf)): −60 V
V<sub>DS</sub>, ±20 V V<sub>GS</sub>, AEC-Q101, TO-252 (same footprint family
as the selected QP). I<sub>DSS</sub> at V<sub>GS</sub> = 0, **V<sub>DS</sub> =
−60 V**: 1 µA max at 25 °C, **50 µA max at T<sub>J</sub> = 125 °C**, 150 µA at
175 °C. That is a hotter-specified, lower maximum than the selected part's
70 µA at 125 °C and −32 V. R<sub>DS(on)</sub> 15.5 mΩ at −10 V, 20.0 mΩ at
−4.5 V; V<sub>GS(th)</sub> −1.5 to −2.5 V.

So the clamp change is a **choice, not forced**. Option (c) is materially
cheaper than stated, and it is the option that keeps the working inherited
part the packet told the engineer to reuse (D-REUSE-02, D-POWER-03):

- **Benefit:** the 1.5KE30A's 25.6 V standoff survives a sustained 24 V jump
  start or an alternator overcharge up to about that voltage, which is
  exactly the exposure D-OVERVOLT-04 had to defer. A higher clamp also
  absorbs much less energy from a long, partly suppressed surge than a
  27.7 V clamp does. And it restores the in-vehicle precedent: keyless runs
  the same LM2678 + 1.5KE30A pair in this car.
- **Cost to evaluate, not assumed closed:** QI/QH/QMON/QRESET need a
  ≥60 V V<sub>CEO</sub> BJT with a usable off-state leakage figure, because the
  41.4 V clamp exceeds the 2N3904's 40 V (an existing defect that `DESIGN.md`
  §4 already recorded). The A5 second stage must be redone for a ~43.5 V hot
  clamp: at 330 Ω the BZX55C22 would carry about 56 mA and dissipate about
  1.4 W against 0.5 W, so R_AON and/or the zener change, re-checking the A5
  dropout floor and the series drop at 6 V. The LM2678 goes back to the
  keyless in-service margin (40 V operating, 45 V absolute) rather than
  10.8 V of headroom. Footprints are unchanged or similar (TO-252, TO-92,
  axial), so this does not stop placement.

**Requested:** re-run §3.1/§3.2/§3.4 with SQD50P06-15L (or an equivalent
≥60 V part with a hot I<sub>DSS</sub> maximum) included. Either adopt
option (c) with the A5 stage and BJTs re-checked, or keep the 1.5KE20A with
a reason other than "forced". Update the ledger row for QP. Decide before
the power-entry area is routed.

### R12-REV-2 — name the exposure accurately (documentation)

L04 and handoff §1/§5.3 call the exclusion "a 24 V jump start". The actual
limit is **any sustained P12 above the 17.1 V standoff**, which includes an
alternator/regulator overcharge fault with no operator error. Restate it that
way, as D-OVERVOLT-04 records. Moot if R12-REV-1 adopts option (c), but then
the new sustained ceiling (about 25.6 V) should be stated the same way.

### R12-REV-3 — deterministic UUIDs (requested before owner placement)

`tools/gen_logger_sch.py` assigns fresh random UUIDs on every run. Once a
`.kicad_pcb` exists, every regeneration breaks KiCad's symbol-to-footprint
links. Derive them deterministically so that an unchanged parts table
reproduces the schematic byte for byte.
