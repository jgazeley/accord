## Electrical limits retained

  12–16 V laboratory supply specification is not vehicle qualification.
- Capacitor effective value, bias/aging/ESR and buck stability remain R03.
  Conditional COUT plus CIN decay screening can exceed the 5 s target; only a
  complete-circuit analysis/test can close it. Do not accept isolated RC results.
- The proposed QP/DZ pair needs the new asymmetric gate envelope, actual off-bias
  leakage and inrush/SOA evidence. The manufacturer's +5/-16 V absolute rating
  and condition-specific IDSS support the return's warning; the old 18 V
  criterion cannot be inherited. [Infineon IPD50P04P4L-11 datasheet](https://www.infineon.com/assets/row/public/documents/10/49/infineon-ipd50p04p4l-11-datasheet-en.pdf).
- UOE's proposed 74AHC1G17GV operates from 2 V; this is compatible only under
  the specified supply/reset envelope and still needs ramp/startup review.
  [Nexperia 74AHC/AHCT1G17 datasheet](https://assets.nexperia.com/documents/data-sheet/74AHC_AHCT1G17.pdf).
- Parked totals remain mixed-condition screens with unclosed discrete, logic-input
  and LDO low-load limits. Five energized signal paths still require upstream
  isolation and analog acceptance, coordinated with LRA-03/04. DOR thermal,
  forward-drop and USB recovery are also pending. These are R03–R05 plus bench
  work, not firmware fixes.
